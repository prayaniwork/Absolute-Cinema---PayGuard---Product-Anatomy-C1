# PayGuard research plan: summary so far

Started 2026-10-16 with `/molades-research-plan`. Everything here is `assumed`. Nothing has been tested with a real person yet.
Status: in progress. Next step: finish the kill criterion (Step 4).

## Hypothesis (contrast pair)
**Before (a product, cannot be wrong):**
"One place where the freelancer and client can see the files, share status and skip WhatsApp for approval."

**After (a belief about people):**
Freelance designers (brand, UI/UX, product, social) with 5 to 15 individual or small-business clients get paid late after they have already delivered. About half the time (5 in 10) it is because the client is waiting on someone else's money. The other half (4 in 10 from unclear agreement on the work, 1 in 10 from an unhappy client) comes from not agreeing clearly on what the work was. The cost to the designer is time and effort already spent with no money in.

## The owner's guess at why payments are late (to be checked)
Out of 10 late payments:
- **A. Client waiting on someone else's money:** 5
- **B. Client unhappy with the design:** 1
- **C. Unclear agreement on the work:** 4

What this means for the product: roughly half the delay (A) is something PayGuard cannot fix; it can only make it visible and give it a date. The other half (B and C) is where approval before payment and a clear scope can help.

```
For:             freelance designers (brand, UI/UX, product, social), 5-15 clients
Explicitly not:  photographers (they prepay); agencies and large companies
In:              late payment after delivery; unclear agreement on the work
```

**AARRR stage: Retention.** Reason given: if a designer doesn't get paid on the first project, they drop off. Open risk logged: this assumes Activation works, meaning the client opens the link and goes along with it. If clients won't, retention is never reached.

## Kill criterion (not finished)
Draft: "If fewer than 6 of 10 designers say ___, I stop."
Still needed before it can fire: what they must *describe* (a real event, not a wish), the time window (for example the last 3 months), and that it is said unprompted. At least one criterion must also kill the cause (the "because"), not only the problem.
