# Trust, payments and compliance review (trust-risk)

## 1. Verdict
The UI says "secured, held, released" while the PRD (Phase 1) and the locked decision say no escrow, so the core promise is unsupported. No document is a legal tax invoice, silence/refund/non-delivery/forwarded-link have no rules, and a first-time client pays a surprise 6.49% to an unnamed holder. **[LEGAL]** = needs counsel or a CA, not design.
Scores: promise accuracy 3 | fee/document transparency 4 | compliance readiness 2 | failure-path design 2 | client checkout trust 4 | privacy/consent 3 | terms legibility 6.

## 2. What is working: keep it
- Itemised checkout (`client-checkout`) ties out: 38,000 + 2,090 + 376 = 40,466; three separate documents with payout hidden from the client.
- "What you're agreeing to, in plain words" (`setup`): right pattern; add the legal text behind it.
- Two-step approve with "It can't be undone."; "View only until you approve" (`client-review`).
- "Card details go straight to the payment provider. PayGuard never sees them." is the best trust sentence in the product.

## 4. Rethink / bold bets
1. **Pick the money model, then one vocabulary.** A "Where is the money" strip (You > Held by [partner] > Meera, or You > Meera) on checkout, review and Payments.
2. **"Rules of this project" as an object**: fees, reply window, silence rule, rounds, refund rule, rights. Set once, shown on invite, accepted with a code, linked from every deadline.
3. **Compliance-grade document set**: advance receipt, tax invoice (work), tax invoice (PayGuard fee, GST split), TDS note.

## 5. Top 5 fixes, in order
1. Decide direct-pay vs held funds; rewrite every released/secured/protected sentence (F1, F12, F13).
2. Write and show silence, refund and non-delivery rules on invite, checkout, review (F2, F3, F9).
3. One-time-code step-up at Pay and Approve; approval bound to named files (F7, F8).
4. Fix price/GST/tax presentation: no "(example)", all-in price on invite, GST on both fees, legal profile and payout KYC, TDS note (F4-F6).
5. Checkout trust block and failure states (F10, F11).
