## Today
**Job of the page:** "What do I have to work on today, where is my attention needed, what do I have to submit." A very clear board of today's work. Monday.com's dashboard is the reference for showing what is to do, what is going on, and what is done.

1. **Needs your attention is framed wrongly.** Today the row starts with the client name ("Northwind Cafe"), so the first instinct is "the client needs attention". The real reason is: payment is 4 days late, or the advance is 5 days late, and **I** need to nudge or remind them. Lead with the reason and my action, then the client.
2. **Payment status:** keep as is.
3. **Waiting on clients:** not a dropdown. Idea: a **bell icon at the top** that shows who I need to remind and where the wait is.
4. **Coming up:** not a dropdown. A calendar-style view or a plain scroll.
5. A clear board of today's work, in the Monday.com spirit (what is open, what is in motion, what is done).
- **?** What are the columns of the "board"? **?** Where does the bell sit and what does it show?

4. **Body = a vertical timeline of deliverables:**

**Step 3: Terms**

- The **"Review the contract" button goes at the end of the steps** (bottom), not at the top or in the summary.

## Answers to the questionnaire (23 Oct 2026)
1. **Today board columns:** To do / In progress / Done.
2. **Bell icon:** header icon with a count; opens a list with a Remind button per client.
3. **Coming up:** "a horizontal calendar, or how Monday.com has tasks for later". Working reading: a horizontal strip of the next 7 days (click a day to see its tasks) plus a "Later" group, like Monday.com. To confirm.
4. **Client with two projects:** two timelines stacked on the same client page.
5. **Payment split:** the split (one payment / half and half / three payments) **replaces** the advance setting. First slice is the advance; the rest are paid on approval.
6. **Logo initials:** the client's name.
7. **Rename:** Stages becomes Deliverables everywhere (both sides, contract, statuses).
8. **Search:** remove the visible search item and the Cmd+K shortcut.
